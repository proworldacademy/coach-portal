Standards
Look — high-end: clean grade, natural light, steady camera, no filters. Coach tips can be phone-shot; everything the photographer touches is cinematic.
Brand — black/white base, lime #E2FE78 as the accent; titles in Review Condensed, uppercase italic; body in Aeonik; real PROWORLD wordmark on the end card.
Copy — "personalized", never "boutique". Say what ProWorld does; no comparisons with other academies. Academics are not offered yet — leave them out.
Formats — Reels 9:16, 30–60s, hook in the first 2 seconds, captions burned in. Carousels 4:5, 5 slides. Stories: real, fast, daily.

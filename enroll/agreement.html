Program policies & enrollment agreement.

What a family agrees to when enrolling at ProWorld Academy, Delray Beach: how the monthly plan is billed, what happens around school breaks, what happens when a payment is late, and how to cancel. Plain words, the same rules for everyone.

Billing

The 1st of the monthEvery monthly plan is charged on the 1st, for that month, to the card saved on Stripe at enrollment. One date for everyone.
Starting mid-monthAt enrollment you pay only the remaining training days of that month: the monthly price × remaining training days ÷ weekdays in the month, rounded to the dollar. The full monthly price applies from the next 1st.
School breaksThe academy follows the Palm Beach County school calendar. Thanksgiving break, winter break, spring break and the summer are not program weeks. A month touched by a break is billed by the same formula (training days ÷ weekdays), the credit shows on the invoice, and the family is told before the 1st. June and July are not billed. Camps during those weeks are enrolled separately.
Changing the planA change (more days, fewer days, another program) is prorated the same way for the rest of the month; the new price applies from the next 1st.
One-time itemsDrop-ins, flex packs, lesson packages, private lessons, camps, court reservations and weekly plans are charged when booked, never on the 1st.

If a payment is late

Day 1 to 5If the 1st's charge does not go through, Stripe retries the card and emails a link to update it. Nothing else changes.
The 6thA $50 late fee is added if the month is still unpaid after the 5th.
The 10thTraining pauses until the balance is paid; the attendance list shows the hold. Training resumes as soon as the payment is in.
The 15thThe enrollment is cancelled and the spot is released. Coming back means paying the balance including the late fee and re-enrolling, prorated from the return date.

Cancelling

Before the 1stA monthly plan can be cancelled any time before the 1st — from the family's Stripe account (the link on the thank-you page and in every receipt) or through the front desk. It takes effect at the end of the month already billed. The current month is not refunded.

Attendance, lessons and courts

AttendancePlayers are expected on their enrolled days. A session attended on a day not on the plan is charged at the program's drop-in rate to the card on file, unless the front desk has approved it as a swap. Missed sessions are not credited.
Flex packsSold for Full Time and Afterschool only; sessions are booked through the front desk at least 6 hours ahead and expire at the end of the term.
Private lessonsPaid in advance; 12 hours' notice to cancel without charge.
Court reservations$20 an hour on hard and clay courts for non-members; enrolled families reserve at no charge. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before.
WeatherWhen the academy cancels a session for weather, the session is made up or credited; the front desk confirms by text.
Late pick-up$30 per 30 minutes after the end of the program day.

Your details

What we keepThe player's name and date of birth, and the parent's name, email and mobile — for the roster, the coaches' reports, receipts and schedule texts. Card details are entered on Stripe's secure page and never reach the academy. Questions about your data or your account: info@proworldacademy.com or (561) 403-0008.
